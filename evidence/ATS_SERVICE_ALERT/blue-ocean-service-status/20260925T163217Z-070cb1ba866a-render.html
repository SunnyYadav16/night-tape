Product Update: Passive Orders Outside Price Bands, Monday, July 20 Session  

To improve Subscribers’ user experience, Blue Ocean ATS will begin accepting passive orders priced outside the 20% price reference bands into the order book. This change takes effect for the Monday, July 20 session, which begins Sunday, July 19 at 8:00 PM ET. 

Previously, these orders were rejected at entry. Going forward:

  • Passive orders outside the price bands will be accepted and maintained in a non-displayed state
  • These orders will not be marketable or eligible for execution unless modified to a price within the bands
  • These orders will not be included in Blue Ocean market data feeds
  • Subscribers may cancel or replace these orders at any time. A replacement priced within the bands becomes eligible for normal display and execution
  • Orders remaining outside the bands will be treated identically to all other orders and automatically cancelled back at 4:00 AM ET

No other changes to order types or market functionality are occurring at this time. No order entry or market data specifications are changing as part of this update, and no technology changes are required for Subscribers.

Please contact Blue Ocean Support with any questions at support@blueoceanats.com.

Best regards,

Blue Ocean Technologies LLC

2:00 PM ET 2025-03-05: Daylight Savings Time Change

This is a reminder that the Eastern Time zone will be changing from Eastern Standard Time to Eastern Daylight Time on March 09, 2024. At 2:00 am EST (06:00 UTC), clocks will leap forward to 3:00 AM EDT (06:00 UTC).

Blue Ocean ATS follows the Eastern Time zone for market hours which will also change as a result.

The Monday, March 10 trading session will therefore start at 00:00 UTC, one hour earlier than previous sessions, and end at 08:00 UTC, also one hour earlier.

For reference, the Blue Ocean ATS trading hours in local time zones will be as follows:

Japan, Korea: 9:00 am to 5:00 pm

Hong Kong, Singapore: 8:00 am to 4:00 pm

London: 12:00 am to 8:00 am

The next time change moving Eastern Daylight Time to Eastern Standard Time will be November 2, 2025.
